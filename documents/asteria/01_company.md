# Asteria Systems (fictional teaching example)

Asteria Systems sells industrial inspection software to factories. Its products are sold through annual subscriptions. This document packet is fictional and is only for demonstrating the committee workflow.

At the end of FY2025, Asteria reported 920 enterprise customers, compared with 790 at the end of FY2024. Net revenue retention was 112% in FY2025.

Management says a new machine-vision product entered general availability in January 2026. No audited revenue contribution from that product is available in this packet.

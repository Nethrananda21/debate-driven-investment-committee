# Asteria Systems risk notes (fictional)

Asteria's three largest customers accounted for 31% of FY2025 revenue. One of those contracts is due for renewal in September 2026.

The company reported that two competitors introduced lower-priced inspection software in 2025. The packet does not contain a verified measure of customer losses to those competitors.

Asteria plans $28 million of capital expenditure in FY2026, compared with $16 million in FY2025. Management has not provided a funded plan for this increase in this packet.
